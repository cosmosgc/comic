# VIP / Premium Subscription Ideas

Possible benefits for a future paid subscription. Treat these as ideas to
validate with users, not committed product requirements.

## Reader experience

- Optional early access to scheduled comics, with the release window clearly
  shown and the same access rules applied consistently across the site.
- Custom reading themes, background colors, and additional reader controls.
- More bookmarks, reading lists, and saved filters.

## Community and personalization

- A profile VIP badge and optional profile accent or theme.
- More profile customization, such as extra pinned comics or collections.
- Additional notification controls, including alerts for followed comics and
  creators.
- Supporter recognition that can be hidden by the subscriber.

## Convenience

- Advanced library filters and sorting, including unread, recently updated,
  and followed comics.
- Cross-device reading progress synchronization and more detailed reading
  history.
- Optional downloadable reading lists or offline access, subject to creator
  permissions and copyright requirements.

## Subscription guidelines

- Keep basic browsing, reading, and community participation available without
  payment.
- Clearly disclose benefits, renewal terms, cancellation steps, and any
  limitations before checkout.
- Avoid giving paid accounts moderation privileges or access to private user
  data.
- Make supporter recognition and optional profile effects opt-in.
- Confirm creator and rights-holder permission before including exclusive
  content or downloads in a subscription.
